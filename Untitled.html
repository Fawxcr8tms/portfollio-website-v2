ABOUT ME

I'm Njabulo Mkhize, also known as JB — a multidisciplinary creative with 7+ years working across graphic design, illustration, motion graphics, and 3D modelling.

My work lives at the intersection of modern digital craft and raw creative expression, where precision meets the fluid world of illustration and 3D space.

Driven by curiosity and a simple belief — every challenge has a solution — I approach each project thinking critically, working boldly, and never settling for the obvious answer.

In a field that never stands still, neither do I. I'm constantly evolving, embracing new tools and techniques to bring fresh perspectives to everything I create.
